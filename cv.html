Research Experience

Quantified regimes of stability and rounding-induced chaos. (paper, code)
  • Robust Vision Transformers: Understanding and Mitigating Representation Vulnerabilities (ISVC 2025): Examined how small input changes affect layer-wise representations in Vision Transformers. Proposed NeuroShield-ViT to neutralize vulnerable neurons and enhance adversarial robustness without retraining, raising PGD-100 accuracy from 15.6% to 72.3%. Classification accuracy also improved by 71.6% against IGO attacks on ImageNet1K. (paper)
  • Adversarial Exploitation in Robot Vision-Language Navigation (IROS 2024): Developed a vision-language navigation (VLN) framework for embodied robotic agents, analyzing perception-to-action pipelines within vision-language-action (VLA) architectures. Studied how representation shifts propagate through navigation policy networks, affecting task planning and real-world embodied decision making. Achieved 91.7% landmark-conditioned path redirection and proposed a noise-sensitivity-based detection mechanism improving navigation policy stability by 96.3%. (paper, code)
  • ActivationGuard: Prompt Injection Detection in LLM Agents (under review): Lightweight detector on frozen LLM hidden states with logistic regression; expanded training coverage raised attack recall from 17.2% to 93.8% under 5% FPR validation budget - at 0.979 AUROC.

Enhanced 3D scene understanding for downstream robotic navigation and manipulation, improving visual spatial reasoning benchmark performance by 2.1%. (paper)

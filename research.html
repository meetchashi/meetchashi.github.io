Research

My research focuses on robustness and explainability of large multimodal models — understanding how microscopic input perturbations cascade through neural representations to cause failures in vision-language understanding, embodied navigation, and LLM reasoning.

ActivationGuard: Prompt Injection Detection in LLM Agents
Under review

Frozen LLM hidden states, logistic regression · 2026

Built a lightweight detector for indirect prompt injection in network-integrated LLM agents using logistic regression on frozen LLM hidden states. Expanded training coverage raised attack recall from 17.2% to 93.8% under a 5% false-positive-rate validation budget, achieving 0.979 AUROC.

Adversarial Attacks on LLMs via Regularized Relaxation
Information Sciences 2026

Co-developed regularized continuous optimization of adversarial suffix embeddings with mapping to valid vocabulary tokens. Improved attack success across five LLMs while running approximately 100 times faster than greedy coordinate gradient (GCG) search.

Instruction Strategies for Tool-Grounded AI Agents
AgentSkills 2026 · Poster

MCP, agent evaluation · ACM CAIS 2026 AgentSkills Workshop

Evaluated instruction strategies in 180 cybersecurity-agent runs across four guidance levels. Increasing context from 591 to 36,001 tokens produced no significant success-rate gain, suggesting that additional procedural guidance may have limited value when the tool environment already supplies useful feedback.

Neural Expansion: A Unified Mechanism for How Deep Neural Networks Generalize
NeurIPS 2026

Accepted at NeurIPS 2026

Proposed Neural Expansion, a geometric account of generalization based on Generalization Intervals, directions along which predictions remain unchanged. Stable regions grow during training; a theoretical bound connects interval size to classification margin and directional sensitivity.

Numerical Instability and Chaos in Large Language Models
IJCNN 2026 · Oral

International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN 2026), part of IEEE WCCI 2026

Discovered that microscopic floating-point input perturbations cause up to 10-million-fold amplification through LLM layers, collapsing logit margins and triggering unpredictable token flips. Quantified regimes of stability and rounding-induced chaos to characterize when LLM predictions remain stable or become unpredictable.

NeuroShield-ViT: Robust Vision Transformers via Representation Vulnerability Mitigation
ISVC 2025 · Oral

International Symposium on Visual Computing (ISVC 2025)

Examined how small input changes affect layer-wise representations in Vision Transformers (ViTs). Proposed NeuroShield-ViT to neutralize vulnerable neurons and enhance adversarial robustness without retraining, raising PGD-100 accuracy from 15.6% to 72.3%. Classification accuracy also improved by 71.6% against IGO attacks on ImageNet1K.

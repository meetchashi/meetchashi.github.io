abstract: >-
  We developed a lightweight prompt-injection detector using logistic regression on frozen LLM hidden states.
  Expanded training coverage raised attack recall from 17.2% to 93.8% under a 5% false-positive-rate
  validation budget, achieving 0.979 AUROC without enlarging the inference architecture.
  Content detection alone does not establish end-to-end agent safety.
